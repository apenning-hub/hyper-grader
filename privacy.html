privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

the short version

  • Everything you enter is stored on your Mac or iPad.
  • Dictation runs on the device. Recordings never leave it.
  • Feedback is written on the device by Apple Intelligence unless you choose the Claude API.
  • If you choose Claude, the app asks first, and student names are replaced before anything is sent.
  • Your Claude API key is kept in the Keychain. We never see it.
  • No tracking, analytics or telemetry. We don't run any servers.

introduction

Hyper Grader is designed with your privacy as a first principle. This policy explains exactly what the app does, and doesn't do, with your data.

data storage

Local only

All courses, assessments, rubrics, grading records and student names are stored on your Mac or iPad, in the app's standard container. None of this data is uploaded to any server, because we don't operate any servers.

Speech recognition

Voice transcription runs entirely on the device using Apple's Speech framework. The app refuses to dictate if on-device recognition isn't available, rather than sending audio to a server. Transcripts are stored with your grading records, on the device.

API key storage

If you use the Claude API, your key is stored in the Keychain with system-level encryption. We never see it.

feedback and ai

On-device AI (the default)

Feedback is written by Apple Intelligence on your device. When you use it, nothing leaves your Mac or iPad.

Claude API (optional)

You can choose the Claude API from Anthropic instead. The first time you do, Hyper Grader explains what will be shared and asks your permission. If you decline, it stays on the on-device model. You can withdraw permission at any time with Stop Sharing in Preferences, and the app will ask again before sending anything.

With permission given, the following is sent to Anthropic when you generate feedback or run a consistency check:

  • Rubric criteria and your ratings
  • Your notes or voice transcripts, if any
  • Your feedback style settings
  • For a consistency check, the feedback and scores being compared

Student names are never sent. Before any data leaves your device, the app replaces each name with a placeholder such as "the student" or "Student 2". In a consistency check, the names are added back on your device when the results return. Anthropic never receives identifying information about individual students.

This data is used only to generate feedback. Anthropic's privacy policy governs the transmission: anthropic.com/privacy.

canvas and other files

Hyper Grader doesn't connect to Canvas or any other system. When you import a gradebook export, rubric or class list, or create the files to upload to Canvas, all processing happens on your device. Files are read from and written to places you choose. You upload them to Canvas yourself.

what we don't collect

We do not collect, transmit or store any of the following:

  • Student names or personal information
  • Grading data or feedback content
  • Voice recordings or transcripts
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash reports (only shared with Apple if you opt in via TestFlight)
  • Your location or device identifiers

student data and ferpa

Student names you enter stay on your device. You are responsible for complying with student privacy laws in your jurisdiction (FERPA, COPPA, GDPR and so on). The app works without real names if you'd rather not enter them.

we do not sell your data

We do not sell, share, rent or trade your personal information with any third party for commercial purposes. Since we do not operate any servers and collect no data ourselves, there is nothing to sell.

children's privacy

The app is designed for educators and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

your control over your data

  • Access: all data is in the app's container on your device
  • Delete: uninstalling the app removes all local data
  • Export: export grading records to CSV at any time from the app
  • Questions: email me@apenning.is

gdpr: european users

Because Hyper Grader does not collect or process your personal data on any server, the standard GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability and restriction of processing) are exercised directly within the app. The only third-party processing happens when you explicitly allow the Claude API. In that case, Anthropic acts as a data processor under its own GDPR terms.

changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
